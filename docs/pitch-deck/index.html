02 / Mission Beyond Stage Zero

Why we exist

Australia’s first fully and rapidly reusable launch vehicle — built by students.

Stage Zero is the ground. Everything beyond it is what we’re building toward. From the Creswick Goods Shed, seventeen students design, machine, cast propellant, write range software, and fly hardware.

05 / Roadmap Clear next steps

Mission pipeline

From stand to pad.

  1. Active Engine development 3× B1M engines in build · propellant characterisation · ceramic nozzles
  2. Q3–Q4 ’26 100 m Hopper Drone-power hop — landing arms, handling, propulsive recovery
  3. Q4’26–Q1’27 B1M-01 flight 3 km systems validation · primary near-term mission
  4. 2027 Full-scale STRAVOX 30 km · Mach 2.5 · propulsive landing — depends on B1M outcomes
