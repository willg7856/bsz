## Vehicles & programmes

### STRAVOX (full-scale — in development)
Australia’s first fully and rapidly reusable launch vehicle (programme goal).

**Key specs**
- Height: 2,633 mm
- Diameter: 200 mm
- Base fin span: 520 mm; upper fin span: 340 mm
- Target apogee: **30 km**
- Peak velocity: **Mach 2.5** (at motor burnout, ~10 km AGL)
- Motor: home-built **KNSB** (potassium nitrate / sorbitol), **1,000 mm** grain
- Launch mass: **104.8 kg** (including propellant)
- Landing: **4× T-Motor U13 II** on folding arms; combined landing thrust **112 kgf**; landing T/W **2.05×**
- Airbrake: split / clamshell nosecone that opens after burnout (~3.5× drag area), passively braking from Mach 2.5 to ~64 m/s before landing sequence

**Development stages (as published)**
- STAGE 00 Concept — 2025-Q3 — done
- STAGE 01 Design — 2025-Q4 — done (CAD Rev 4; 23 simulations)
- STAGE 02 Engine Dev. — 2026-Q1–Q2 — **active now** (B1M design done; 3 B1M engines in build)
- STAGE 03 Avionics — 2026-Q3 — upcoming
- STAGE 04 Fabrication — 2026-Q4 — upcoming
- STAGE 05 Testing — 2027-Q1 — upcoming
- STAGE 06 Launch — 2027-Q2 — planning (30 km + propulsive landing demo at CASA-cleared Victorian range)
